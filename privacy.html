Privacy Policy

VolleyScore Board

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VolleyScore Board ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App lets you track volleyball match scores. The following information is stored only on your device (using Apple's standard local storage) and is never transmitted to us or any server we control:

  • Team names you enter and choose to save
  • Match history (scores, duration, timestamps)
  • Your language and display preferences

You can delete this information at any time from within the app (team name management screen and match history screen).

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as device identifiers (e.g. Apple's IDFA), approximate location, and app usage data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before showing personalized ads. Everywhere, the App requests your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any tracking occurs. You can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings app (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

For more information about how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. No Account or Personal Data Collection

The App does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
