Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a team name for later?

Just type a team name and start a match — it's saved automatically. Next time, tap the small arrow next to the team name field to pick it from your saved list.

How do I delete a saved team name?

Tap the arrow next to the team name field, then choose "Manage saved names...". From there you can delete individual names or clear them all.

How do I delete a match from my history?

Open History from the home screen, tap "Edit", then tap the red delete button next to the match you want to remove (or swipe left on it).

Can I change the app's language?

The app automatically matches your device's language if supported. You can also pick a different language manually from the menu on the home screen.
